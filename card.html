Your crew's Card

One Card per crew, kept in your crew's shared folder. Open it, fill in your part, save it back. What you type is also kept on this laptop, so nothing is lost if you have not saved yet.

For a copy to put on screen in your film, use Print the Card and choose Save as PDF as the printer. Your two charts already save as full-size pictures from the chart tools.

In the film

At least one of your charts and your 2040 claim have to appear in your finished film, on screen or spoken. That is a film requirement, not a maths one — but it is easier to plan for now than to fix in the edit.
