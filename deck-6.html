The two words

Correlation — two things move together.

Causation — one of them makes the other happen.

A chart can show you the first. No chart on its own can show you the second.

Hidden variable — a third thing moving both of them, which is not on the chart at all.

Notes — Write all three on the board and leave them there for the rest of the lesson.

Before you believe any chart

  • Where does the vertical axis start?
  • Are the gaps between the numbers the same size?
  • How much of the data is shown, and what got left out?
  • Is there a third thing moving both of these?
  • How far past the measurements does the claim reach?
Notes — These five are the whole unit. If they leave with only this slide, that is a good outcome.
