What stage 7 needs from you

H₂O — Towards 2040 · Mathematics strand · 16/08/2026

Stage 6 is the machinery — the rooms, the checkpoints, the hint ladders. It is being built now and needs none of this. Stage 7 is the six data sets and the answer hashes, and it cannot start without the two items marked with a gold box. Everything else can arrive late without stopping the build.

Blocks stage 7The two that stop everything

  • The six variant themes, confirmed Proposed: rainfall · storage levels · shower length · tank size · household size · catchment area. Tick to accept all six, or cross out and replace. Variant 5 must be the negative relationship (household size against water used per person) — one worked example is already written around it.
  • May I invent the numbers? Twelve checkpoints × six variants is 72 answers, plus six recording sheets, 24 scatterplots and 18 scalings. If yes, I build them shaped like published Melbourne Water and BoM figures, each with a source line, for you to veto. If you have real figures, they win.
If the numbers are invented, the staff sheet says so — the same decision you already made for the drinking-fountain data. Lesson 6 is a lesson about honesty with data, and it cannot be built on quietly invented data.

Needed during stage 7Content

  • Marks on the twelve questions docs/CHECKPOINT_QUESTIONS.md. One decision inside it: whether cp2.1 stays a label-reading question or becomes “which axis does the EV belong on”. It is the one checkpoint whose hint ladder does not work, and I think the question is the problem, not the hint.
  • Keep the “I need a hint” button, or cut it Built but not in the contract. It fires the next hint and costs one attempt, so four presses reach the answer just as four wrong answers do. Without it, guessing is the only way to get help.
  • Four bypass codes One per room, fixed across all students, printed on the staff sheet. Leave blank and I will generate memorable ones.
