Two words you need

Bivariate data is two measurements taken from the same thing at the same time.

The explanatory variable (EV) is the one you choose, change, or that comes first. The response variable (RV) is the one you measure — it is the one that depends on the EV. Your worksheets sometimes call these the independent and dependent variable. They mean the same thing.

The EV always goes on the horizontal axis, along the bottom. The RV goes on the vertical axis, up the side.
