The word for it

Two things can move together without one causing the other. When they move together, that is correlation. When one actually makes the other happen, that is causation. A chart can show you correlation. No chart on its own can show you causation.

Usually there is a third thing moving both of them, and it is not on the chart at all. It is called the hidden variable. Ice creams and drinking fountains have one, and you have almost certainly named it already.

Your own prediction in Lesson 5 has the same problem waiting in it. The line carried on past your data because you drew it that way, not because anything you measured says it keeps going.
