Measure the Card

Author-time only. Never linked from the site and never loaded by a student. Serve the repo over HTTP and open this page.

The printed Card places every block at a fixed top with a fixed height, so a field that overruns its box is clipped rather than grown. The character limits in card.html are what stop that happening, and this is where they come from: it loads the real Card, forces the print rules on, and binary searches the longest string of ordinary prose that still fits each box.

Take the “limit” column, not the “fits” column. Fits is the measured ceiling with this filler text; limit is that with 8% taken off, because a crew writing about litres per shower will not use the same letters.
