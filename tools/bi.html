Two variables together

Put two columns of numbers in, get a scatterplot out. Then place a line through the points and use it to predict.

1 · Your data

One pair per line, separated by a comma, a tab or a space. The first number goes across, the second goes up.

Name both axes and give both units before you save. A chart nobody can read the axes of does not prove anything.

Your line

Drag either end. Or tab to an end and move it with the arrow keys — hold shift to move further.

4 · Use it to predict

Beyond our data

Nobody measured out here, so this is a guess based on the line carrying on. It might not.

You are guessing further than your data is wide. On the chart the line fades out here, because there is less and less behind it.
