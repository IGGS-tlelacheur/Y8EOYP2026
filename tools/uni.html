One variable on its own

One column of measurements, five ways of showing it. Not every way suits every kind of data, which is most of the point.

1 · Your data

One measurement per line. Numbers, or words like yes and no. If you paste numbers with a heading on top, the heading is used as the label. With words, every line counts as an answer — so leave the heading out.

Name what you measured, and give its units, before you save. A chart nobody can read the axis of does not prove anything.

4 · Bin width

How wide each bar is. Wide bars hide the shape; narrow bars show every wobble. Move it and watch what happens.
